Privacy

Last updated 16 October 2026

In short: Match Day keeps your team's squad, games and results so every coach on the team can see them. Use first names only for players. We don't show adverts, sell data or track you around the web. Followers of the live score see team names and scores, never players.

Who we are

Match Day is run by Mark Miles Consultancy Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 08286044; registered office 19 Well Close, Winscombe, England, BS25 1HQ). Contact: markdavidmileswork@gmail.com.

Who decides what goes in

Coaches and their clubs decide which players and details to add to their team. For that team information, the club (or coach) is in charge of it under data protection law and we look after it for them. For coaches' own sign-in details, we're in charge.

What we keep

WhatWhy
Team information: team name, players' first names, line-ups, substitutions, playing time, scores and scorers, fixtures, awards and coaches' notes about playersSo the coaches on the team can run match days and keep a season record
Coaches: the name you choose to show, how you sign in (no account, Google or email), when you last used the app, and your email address if you sign in with Google or emailSo other coaches know who is in the team, and so your teams follow you to a new phone
Join requests: the name you give and how you sign inSo the team's coaches can accept or decline you
Follower link: team names, opponent names, kick-off times, pitches, scores, the match clock and statusSo parents, family and friends can follow the score. It never contains players' names

Coaches' email addresses are never shown to other coaches, parents or anyone else.

Children's information

Please add first names only: no surnames, dates of birth, photos, medical details or contact details. Keep notes about players to football (or rugby): things like "work on staying wide". Only the coaches who have been accepted into the team can see its players. Parents who want to see, correct or remove their child's details should ask the team's coach, or contact us.

Where it's stored

Team information is stored by Google Firebase (Google Cloud) in London. Sign-ins are handled by Firebase Authentication. The app also keeps a copy on each coach's phone so it works without signal. Backups that coaches save are files on their own device, and they're responsible for them.

Who else sees it

  • Google (Firebase) stores the data and handles sign-ins for us.
  • The app's fonts come from Google Fonts, so your phone asks Google's servers for them.

We don't sell data, show adverts or use tracking or analytics cookies. The app stores a few settings on your phone (such as which team is open) so it works; these aren't used to track you.

How long we keep it

Team information stays while the team uses Match Day. A coach can leave a team at any time, which removes them from it and clears the team from their phone. To have a team or your account deleted completely, email us and we'll do it within 30 days. Teams that haven't been used for two years may be deleted after we've tried to contact the coaches.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. Email markdavidmileswork@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Keeping it safe

Each team's information can only be read by the coaches accepted into that team; our security rules enforce this on Google's servers, and are tested before every release. All connections are encrypted.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app.
