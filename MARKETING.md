# Match Day: marketing blurb

WhatsApp-ready (`*bold*` shows as bold). Updated for v0.24.1.

```
⚽🏉 *Match Day – a free touchline app for grassroots coaches*

I've built an app to take the stress out of match days and tournaments, and I'd love coaches to try it this weekend.

*What it does*
• *Line-ups & subs* – tap or drag players on and off, keeper changes included, with a quick undo if you tap the wrong one
• *Fair playing time* – every player's minutes tracked live, so you can see who needs more time
• *Scores & match clock* for every game – one period, halves or quarters
• *Football or rugby* – goals, or tries, conversions and penalties
• *Tournaments* – set up the day's games, then save the results to your season record
• *Season record, awards and notes* – results, scorers, player of the match, and notes to pick up with a player
• *Parent link* – parents follow the live score and fixtures on their phone. No players' names, ever
• *Shared between coaches* – everyone on your team sees the same line-up and subs live. New coaches ask to join and you approve them
• *Works with no signal* at the pitch, and catches up when you're back online

*Free.* Works on iPhone and Android, any team from 3 to 11 a side. First names only (or shirt numbers), so no personal details. Signing in with Google or email is optional but recommended, so your team follows you to a new phone.

*To get started*
1. Open 👉 https://match-day-17b23.web.app
2. iPhone (Safari): Share → *Add to Home Screen*. Android (Chrome): ⋮ → *Install app*
3. Tap *Create a team*, add your team name, players a side and squad
4. Invite your other coaches from the ☰ menu (WhatsApp or the invite code), and share the *Parent link* with your parents' group

It's an early version, so please send me any feedback, bugs or ideas. Save a backup now and then from the ☰ menu while it's in testing.
```
