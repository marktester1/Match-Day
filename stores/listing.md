## Description (both stores; Google allows 4,000 characters, Apple 4,000)

No more shuffling paper and clipboards on the sidelines.

Match Day is the free app for grassroots football and rugby coaches. It runs your team on match day, one game or a whole tournament, so you can concentrate on coaching.

FAIR PLAYING TIME, WITHOUT THE MATHS
Match Day counts every player's minutes as you play, across every game of the day, and lists the bench least time first. You can see at a glance who needs a run out next: no stopwatch, no notebook.

BUILT FOR THE TOUCHLINE
Big buttons and one hand. Everyone starts on the bench: tap your keeper and starters, kick off, and make a sub with two taps. The clock looks after itself, added time included.

EVERY COACH ON THE SAME PAGE
Your assistant sees the same line-up, subs and score live on their own phone. One can make the subs while the other records the goals.

PARENTS FOLLOW FROM ANYWHERE
One link for the team's group chat gives family the live score, kick-off times and results. They never see a child's name.

A SEASON RECORD THAT KEEPS ITSELF
Results, scorers, minutes, awards and match reports build up by themselves, game after game.

ALSO
• Tournaments: fixtures, pitches and kick-off times, pasted straight from an email or WhatsApp
• Halves or quarters, injury time, yellow and red cards
• Rugby: tries, conversions, penalties and the sin bin
• Works with no signal at the pitch, and catches up when you're back online
• First names only: no surnames, photos or dates of birth needed
• No adverts, and nothing is sold
