When you use it

On the day itself, from kick-off to the final whistle

Match Day is built for the day your team plays: one match, or a tournament with several games back to back. Results, scorers and reports then go into your season record.

⚽

A match day

One game. Pick the line-up, kick off, make your subs and record the goals. Everyone's minutes are tracked as you go, and families follow the score live.

🏆

A tournament day

Several games in a day. Put in the fixtures, kick-off times and pitches, run each game, and keep minutes fair across the whole day. Save the results and a match report at the end.

What it does

Everything you juggle on the touchline, in one place

Designed for the touchline: big buttons, one hand, any weather.

🔁

Line-ups and subs

Pick the team, then tap or drag players on and off. Keeper changes included, with a quick undo if you tap the wrong one.

⏱️

Fair playing time

Every player's minutes are tracked live across the day, least first, so you can see who needs a run out.

⚽

Scores and the clock

Goals and who scored, one period, halves or quarters. Rugby too: tries, conversions, penalties and the sin bin.

📣

Followers

One link for family, carers and friends: the live score, kick-off times and results. No sign-up, and never a child's name.

🏆

Tournaments and results

Set up the day's fixtures, then save the results, scorers, cards and a match report to your season record.

📶

Works with no signal

Keeps going at the pitch with no reception and catches up when you're back online. Every coach on the team sees the same thing.

Get started

Ready in two minutes

  1. Open it on your phone or tablet

    Go to Match Day in the browser. On iPhone or iPad (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android (Chrome): ⋮ → Install app.

  2. Create your team

    Add your team name, how many a side, and your squad's first names. Paste them in one go if you like.

  3. Invite and share

    Invite your other coaches from the ☰ menu, and send your follower link to the team's group chat.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

Yes, Match Day is free to use at the moment. If that ever changes, we'll tell you in the app at least 30 days before.

Which phones and tablets does it work on?

Any recent iPhone, iPad, or Android phone or tablet, in the browser. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like any other app. A tablet gives you a bigger pitch to tap on, and it works on computers too.

What if there's no signal at the pitch?

It keeps working. Everything saves on your phone and uploads when you're back in signal, so the other coaches and followers catch up then.

Can more than one coach use it?

Yes. Every coach on the team sees the same line-up, subs and score live. New coaches ask to join with your team's code and you approve them.

Does it do one-off matches as well as tournaments?

Yes. Set up one game for a league match, or several for a tournament day. Results go to your season record either way.

Do I need an account?

No. Signing in with Google or email is optional, but recommended so your team comes with you if you change phone.

Football only?

Football and rugby. Rugby has tries, conversions and penalties, and a sin-bin countdown that still counts towards a player's time on the pitch.

Email markdavidmileswork@gmail.com with feedback, bugs or ideas. It's an early version and every suggestion is read.
