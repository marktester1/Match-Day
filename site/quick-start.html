Quick start

Everything you need for your first match day, in eight steps. For the detail, see the full guide.

  1. Open Match Day on your phone

    Go to app.matchday-app.uk. There's nothing to download.

    Put it on your Home Screen so it opens like any other app. iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen.

  2. Create your team

    Tap Create a team, type your name (other coaches see it) and follow the set-up:

    • your team's name, e.g. Riverside U10s
    • how many a side
    • your players' first names only: type them or paste a list

    Choose Quick start to be ready in under a minute, or Full set-up to add your games, opponents and kick-off times now.

  3. Invite your other coaches

    Open the ☰ menu and tap Invite on WhatsApp (or copy the invite). They open the link, tap Ask to join, and you accept them in the ☰ menu. Everyone then sees the same line-up, subs and score, live.

  4. Share the score with supporters

    In the ☰ menu, tap Create a supporters' link and send it to the team's group chat. Family can follow the live score and fixtures. They never see players' names.

  5. Pick the team and kick off

    On the day, tap your game. Everyone starts on the bench. Tap GK for your keeper and Start for the rest of your starting team, tap Out for anyone who isn't there today (once, for the whole day), and tap Kick off. The clock starts and everyone's minutes are counted.

  6. Subs, goals and cards

    • Sub: tap a player on the pitch, then the player coming on. Or drag one onto the other.
    • Goal: tap + on your score, then who scored. Tap + on theirs for the opposition.
    • Card: tap the player, then the card.
    • Tapped the wrong thing? Tap Undo.

    The bench is listed least time first, so you can see at a glance who needs a run out.

  7. Half time and full time

    When the half's time is up, the clock carries on into added time by itself. Tap Half time when the referee blows, and Start 2nd half to carry on. At the end, tap Full time.

  8. Save the day's results

    When you tap Full time on your last game, tap Save today's results. Scores, scorers and everyone's minutes go into your season record, and the app shows you where (Season → Results). Saved too soon? Undo puts the day back. You can add a short match report and share the results with parents.
