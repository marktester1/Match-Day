Getting started

Open the app

Match Day runs in your phone's browser: go to app.matchday-app.uk. It works on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and tablets, and computers. There's nothing to download or pay.

Tip: add it to your Home Screen so it opens full screen with one tap. iPhone and iPad: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: in Chrome tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Start a new team

Type your name (the other coaches see it, so they know who you are) and tap Create a team. You become the team's manager. The set-up then asks how much you want to do now:

  • Quick start: your team's name, how many a side, and your players. Ready in under a minute.
  • Full set-up: also how many games you're playing, how long each lasts, halves or quarters, and your opponents, kick-off times and pitches.

Everything is saved as you go, so if you're interrupted you carry on where you left off. You can change all of it later. Do this later skips the set-up altogether.

Join a team someone else made

Ask a coach for the team's invite code or link. Open the app, type your name and the code, and tap Ask to join. A coach in the team accepts you, and the team opens on your phone.

Coaching more than one team

Open the ☰ menu and tap + Add a team. Each team has its own squad, games, season and invite code. Switch between them from the team name at the top or from My teams in the menu.

Your squad

Your players live in Season → Squad. Every coach on the team sees the same squad.

  • Type a player's first name and add them, or use Add several at once to paste a list, one per line or separated by commas.
  • Surnames are dropped for you. If two players share a first name, the app adds their surname's first letter.
  • To remove a player, tap Remove next to them, then tap again to confirm. Their minutes and goals already saved in results stay.

Team settings

Also in Squad, under Team settings: your team's full name, the shorter scoreboard name (what's shown on each game and in results, and what the app looks for when you paste fixtures), how many a side you usually play, and football or rugby.

Setting up a match day

Go to Tournament → Games. If you've played before, the app asks Next match day: same as last time?: tap Yes, same settings and you're done.

Otherwise, under Set up your games, choose:

  • Games: 1 for a single match, or how many you'll play at a tournament (up to 10).
  • Minutes a game and two halves or four quarters (or one period).
  • A side: how many players each team has on the pitch. You can change it for a single game too.
  • A tournament name and date, if you like.

Opponents, kick-off times and pitches

Tap Enter opponents and kick-off times and fill in each game, or tap Paste fixtures and paste the fixture list from an email or WhatsApp. The app picks out your games (it looks for your scoreboard name), with their times, opponents and pitches. Line-ups and scores are kept if you paste again. Tap Save fixtures to go back to all your games.

For a single match there's a Home | Away switch. Away games show the other team first on the scoreboard and for supporters.

Picking the team

Tap a game to open it. Everyone starts on the bench, because everyone who turns up plays or is a sub. Under Who's playing, each player has four buttons:

  • GK: your goalkeeper
  • Start: starting on the pitch
  • Bench: a sub
  • Out: not here today

Out is for the whole day: mark a player Out once and they're out of every game that hasn't kicked off. If they turn up after all, tap Bench and they're back on the bench for those games.

The app tells you how many more you need for the game's size. If you start with a different number, it asks before kick-off, so a short-handed or bigger game is a choice, not a mistake.

Late arrival? Use Add to bench on the game: type their first name and they join the bench (and the squad).

When you're ready, tap Kick off. Line-ups are picked separately for each game, so you can share minutes around across a tournament.

During a game

Subs

Tap a player on the pitch, then the bench player coming on. Or tap a bench player first and then who's coming off, or drag one onto the other. The bench is listed least time first, so you can see who needs a run out. Undo last sub takes back the last one.

Changing the keeper

Drag the keeper onto another player to swap the gloves, or give the gloves to someone from the pop-up when you tap the keeper.

Goals

Tap + on your team's score, then tap who scored (or Own goal). Tap + on the other team's score for theirs. − takes a goal off if you tapped by mistake.

Cards

Tap the player, then 🟨 Yellow card or 🟥 Red card. A player sent off can't come back on, and the team plays one short. To take a card back, remove it from the list under the pitch.

Undo

Most actions show an Undo button for a few seconds afterwards. Use it straight away if you tapped the wrong thing.

Two coaches, one game: every coach on the team sees the same game live, so one can make subs while another records goals. Changes appear on everyone's phone within a second or two.

The clock, half time and added time

The clock starts at kick-off and keeps going even if you lock your phone or switch apps.

  • When a half's time is up, the clock carries on into added time by itself. It shows the full time for the half with the added time underneath, e.g. 10:00 and +1:30 added time. You don't need to do anything.
  • When the referee blows, tap Half time (or End of Q1 and so on with quarters). The next half starts from its usual time, whatever added time was played.
  • If the referee blows early, tap Half time then too: the clock moves to the end of the half.
  • Tap Start 2nd half when play restarts.
  • At the end, tap Full time. If the clock is short of the full length, you can end at the time shown or at the full length.

Goals in added time are shown like the referee writes them: 10+2'. Players' minutes include added time.

Forgot to tap Full time? A clock left running stops by itself an hour after the game should have ended, so nothing is lost.

Pause stops the clock for a stoppage, and Resume starts it again.

Fixing mistakes

  • Wrong time on a goal or sub: each one in the game's lists has its time from kick-off. Type a new one (like 12:30) to correct it.
  • Clock wrong: tap Edit on the game, then Change game length or fix the clock. Subs recorded after the new time move back to it, so playing time stays right.
  • Forgot to record a sub: in Edit, use Add a sub that already happened.
  • Ended the game too soon: tap Reopen game, or Change match time on a finished game.
  • Start a game again: Reset this game… clears its score, subs, cards and clock (you can undo it straight afterwards).

Fair playing time

Tournament → Minutes played lists every player's minutes across the whole day, least first, with the average so far. Players who need more time are marked, so you can bring them on next.

Minutes count time on the pitch, including added time and time in the sin bin. They're for the coaches only: supporters never see them.

Season → Results also shows season minutes across every saved match day.

Notes

Tournament → Notes is for things to pick up with players, like "work on staying wide". Add a note against a player, and tick it when it's done. Every coach on the team sees the notes, and they stay when a tournament is saved.

Keep notes about the sport and kind: parents can ask to see what's held about their child.

After the day: results and reports

When your last game is over, go to Games and tap Finish tournament and save to Results (tap again to confirm). This saves the scores, scorers, cards and every player's minutes to Season → Results, then clears the games ready for next time.

Match report

You're offered a match report straight away: highlights, what went well, what to work on. Or tap Write it later and add it from Results whenever you like.

Sharing results

Open the match day in Results:

  • Share results sends scores only, ready for a group chat.
  • Share with names adds scorers, cards and the match report, using first names. Only use it where your club is happy for children to be named.

Correcting a saved match day

Tap Edit on a saved match day to fix an opponent, score, scorer or goal time.

Awards and the season

Season → Awards: after training or a match, give awards like player of the match. Pick 2 or 3 players and say what for. The list shows fewest awards first, so nobody gets missed.

Season → Results shows every saved match day, every scorer this season and everyone's season minutes.

At the end of the season, save a backup, then use Delete all season data in Results to start the new season fresh. The squad stays until you remove players.

Supporters

Family, carers and friends can follow the live score from anywhere, with no app or sign-up.

  1. Open the ☰ menu and tap Create a supporters' link.
  2. Tap Share on WhatsApp (or copy the link) and send it to the team's group chat.

Supporters see team names, the live score and match clock (including added time), half time and full time, and the day's fixtures and results. They never see players' names, line-ups or minutes, and they can't change anything.

Alerts on their phone

Supporters can tap Get notifications and choose kick-off, goals, cards, half time and full time. On iPhone they first add the page to their Home Screen and open it from there.

A new season or a link that's spread too far

Each link works for one season. Make a new one in the menu whenever you like: the old link stops updating.

Coaches and managers

Inviting coaches

In the ☰ menu, under Invite other coaches, tap Invite on WhatsApp or Copy invite. The code only sends a request: someone in the team has to accept each person, from the menu or the alert on their phone. Only accept people you know, as they'll see and change the squad and games.

Managers

The coach who made the team is its manager, and can make up to four more coaches managers. Managers can remove coaches and make a new invite code (the old one stops working at once: useful if it's been shared too widely).

If you're the manager and want to leave, the app asks who takes over first. If a team ever has no manager, any coach can tap Become the manager.

Alerts for coaches

Turn on notifications in the menu to hear when someone asks to join, or when you've been accepted into a team. On iPhone, open Match Day from your Home Screen first.

Your account and phones

You can use Match Day without an account: your teams are then tied to that phone. If the phone is lost or its browser data is cleared, you'd lose them, so we recommend signing in:

  • Continue with Google, or
  • Create an account with email (any email address and a password).

Your teams are then saved to your account. On a new phone, open the app and tap Sign in to get them all back. Other coaches can see you're really you; your email is never shown to them.

Sign out on this phone and Leave this team on this phone are in the menu. Anything not yet uploaded is checked first, so you don't lose changes.

No signal

Pitches often have poor signal, so Match Day keeps working without it. Everything saves on your phone first and uploads when there's signal again. The status at the top shows where things stand: Shared · live means everything's uploaded. If something didn't save, tap the red status to try again.

Other coaches and supporters catch up as soon as your phone is back in signal.

Backups

In the ☰ menu, Save a backup saves a copy of everything (squad, games, season record and awards) to your phone as a file. Do it at the end of each season, or before deleting anything.

The file holds your players' first names, so keep it somewhere private and don't share it in group chats.

Restore from a backup shows a preview of what will change before anything happens, and you can undo a restore afterwards.

Rugby

Choose rugby in Squad's team settings (or in the set-up). Then:

  • scores are tries, conversions, penalties and drop goals (or tries only, if your age group counts that way);
  • a yellow card puts a player in the sin bin with a 10-minute countdown, which still counts towards their time on the pitch;
  • there's no goalkeeper to pick.

Keeping children's details safe

  • Use first names only: no surnames, dates of birth, photos or contact details.
  • Only the coaches accepted into your team can see its players.
  • Supporters never see players' names, and playing time is never shared.
  • Only invite coaches you trust, and remove coaches who leave the club.

Read the privacy notice for the details.
